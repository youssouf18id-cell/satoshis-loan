Frequently Asked Questions

Know Before You Apply

Does submitting an application guarantee a loan?

No. An application is only a request. Approval depends on eligibility, legal requirements, and available funds.

Do I need to pay an upfront fee?

This demo does not ask for payment. Any future fee must be lawful, disclosed in advance, and included in the written terms. Do not pay based only on a website form or email.

Can I apply from any country?

The website may be accessible worldwide, but lending cannot automatically be offered in every country. Availability must be checked country by country.

Can I repay in installments?

The form records your preference only. Installments are available only if offered in the final agreement.

Does this website connect to my Bitcoin wallet?

No. Never enter a seed phrase, private key, exchange password, or wallet recovery phrase into a loan application.

How do I contact support?

Email satstack.sathoshi@gmail.com . Do not email sensitive identity documents until a secure and appropriate process has been established.
